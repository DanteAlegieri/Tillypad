
# Restaurant OS 5 — Design System

## Main principles

1. The dashboard answers what happened, why it matters, and what to do.
2. Every KPI includes context and comparison.
3. Missing data is shown honestly as "Нет данных".
4. Critical operational state is visible without opening another page.
5. Legacy dashboard remains available during migration.

## Main dashboard blocks

- Digital Director
- Restaurant Score
- KPI grid
- Quick summary
- Restaurant now
- Attention center
- Period pace and forecast
